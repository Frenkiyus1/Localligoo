What is LOCALINGO?

LOCALINGO is an English learning space where your language meets your local world.

Instead of learning English through unfamiliar situations, LOCALINGO helps you learn through places, people, culture and everyday life around you.

Our core values

01 — Learn: Learn with Purpose

Build real English skills through meaningful and familiar contexts.

02 — Connect: Connect with Your Place

Discover people, culture and places that make your community unique.

03 — Explore: Learn by Exploring

Learn, practice and discover through interactive activities.
