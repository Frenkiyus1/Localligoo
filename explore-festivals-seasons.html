Festivals & Seasons

1. A unique beauty contest for .... COWS

Beauty contests aren’t just for people! In Moc Chau, a green highland in Vietnam, cows get their own pageant called “Miss Dairy Cow”. Can you believe it??

It started in 2004, and yes, the cows really compete! They get checked and judged, and the winner is crowned the most beautiful and healthiest cow around. Guess what, the cows don’t just compete on how much milk they make and how good it tastes. They also get dressed up with ribbons and flowers and then walk down the runway like slay queens!!!. Even better, every winner gets her own number (like a player in a game), and her farmer gets to celebrate with her.

The contest is also a big party for farmers. They meet friends, share tips on caring for cows, and talk with scientists to keep their cows healthy. Healthy cows give better milk!

It is so popular that tourists come just to watch. Imagine going on a trip to see a cow contest! So next time you drink a glass of milk, remember: It might have come from a very fancy cow!

2. A Second Tet!?

Most people celebrate Tết only once a year. But in Mộc Châu, the Hmong people get two!

One is the Hmong New Year, a month before the usual Tết. The other is Independence Day Tết on September 2. It lasts three days, from August 31 to September 2. The reason is that on September 2, 1945, Uncle Hồ read the Declaration of Independence, and the Hmong people loved the day so much that they made it their own special Tết!

People also call it the “Love market.” Why is that? Because on the night of September 1st, thousands of people leave their faraway villages and travel to the town center, especially in Mộc Châu. So many people in one place, just like a giant “market”! And the “love” part? Young boys and girls wear their brightest clothes. Then they play so many fun games together, like tug of war, spin tops, or they also throw soft balls called “Pao” as a way to show love, play traditional games like “tung còn” and pound rice to make rice cakes. Musicians even polish their “khèn” (a bamboo flute) until it shines, just to be ready!

The party lasts three days, and it is so popular that the roads get very busy. A traffic jam made by too much fun!

3. The super special pole at the Hết Chá Festival

The Thái people in Mộc Châu have a very amazing festival called “Hết Chá”. In the middle of the party, there is a very special pole. People believe it works like a bridge between us and the spirits of their ancestors. Yes, you heard that right, a bridge made of bamboo!

The pole is made from a tall bamboo tree, then it gets decorated like a Christmas tree, but way much cooler. People hang birds, fish, frogs, flowers, and tools on it. All of them are made from bamboo, wood, and colorful thread.

But why so many things? They show that the Thái people love nature and rice farming. They also send a big wish to the sky: good weather, happy families, and lots and lots of rice!

The whole village also gathers around the pole. They sing, say thanks to their ancestors, and dance the “xòe dance” together. It’s definitely the biggest village party of the year!
