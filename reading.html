💡 DID YOU KNOW?

The Butterfly Secret of the Thai People!

On a traditional Thai com blouse, the silver buttons are shaped like butterflies, and the number of buttons can tell you if a girl is married!

🦋 An odd number (7, 9, or 11 buttons) means she is single!

🦋 An even number (8, 10, or 12 buttons) means she is married!
