The Bamboo Matcher

Listen carefully. Match the sounds!

How to play

  1. Tap a card to hear the word.
  2. Choose another word with the same ending sound.
  3. Match all 4 pairs to complete the game!

Remember!

Some words end with /s/, while others end with /z/. Listen to the final sound, not just the spelling.
